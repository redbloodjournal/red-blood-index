FINDING YOURSELF THROUGH PURPOSE • STEP 12 OF 12

My Life Thesis — Final Course Worksheet

Complete the two-part life thesis: internal transformation and external contribution. Write with honesty rather than perfection. Return to these answers after 90 days.

Name: ______________________   Date: ______________________

1. What did you believe about purpose when you began the course, and which assumptions changed?

2. Which inherited expectations do you now choose to keep, question, or release?

3. What values will you use to evaluate your choices?

4. Part One — Internal Transformation: What kind of person are you choosing to become?

5. What recurring pattern will you work to correct, and what will you do differently?

6. Part Two — External Contribution: Whom or what do you hope your growth will benefit?

7. What meaningful contribution can you begin without seeking approval or recognition?

8. Write your two-part life thesis in one paragraph. Include both who you will become and what you will contribute.

9. Within the next 30 days, what concrete action will you take?

10. Within 60 days, how will you measure what you have learned or improved?

11. Within 90 days, what contribution or change will you examine?

12. How will you revisit your thesis and revise it when new evidence or responsibilities emerge?

Final Reflection

My life is a classroom; my choices are daily examinations. What I learn must be reflected in how I live and what I contribute.
