Finding Yourself Through Purpose • Step 2 of 12

Step 2 Reflection Worksheet

Good Thoughts, Good Words, Good Deeds

← Return to Step 2

Choose one recent thought, one thing you said, and one action you took. Ordinary moments are often more revealing than dramatic ones.

1. What am I examining?

2. What was my motive?

3. Was I being truthful?

4. Was fear, pride, insecurity, compassion, responsibility, revenge, or approval involved?

5. Who could have been harmed, immediately or later?

6. Who benefited, and did I include myself honestly?

7. What consequence followed?

8. Would I make the same choice again?

9. Would I accept the same behavior if it were done to me?

10. If the outcome showed I was wrong, what would I correct?

11. Who taught me what “good” means?

12. Have I ever consciously examined whether I agree?

Do not try to make the answer sound wise. Make it honest. The purpose is examination, not perfection.
