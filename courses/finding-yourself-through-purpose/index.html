Red Blood Journal Course 1

Finding Yourself Through Purpose

12 Steps to Discover the Life Within. This course does not tell you what your purpose should be. It provides a structured process to examine happiness, identity, conditioning, thoughts, words, actions, values, meaning and the life you choose to live.
