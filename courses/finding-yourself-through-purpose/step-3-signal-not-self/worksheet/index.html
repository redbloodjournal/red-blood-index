The Signal Is Not the Self — Worksheet

This worksheet is not a test. The goal is to observe thoughts without immediately treating them as truth, identity or instruction.

Part A — Observe three thoughts

Thought 1: What appeared?
Did I consciously choose it before it appeared?
Is it mainly a fact, fear, prediction, memory or interpretation?
Where might it have come from?
Thought 2: What appeared?
What emotion accompanies it?
What does it encourage me to do?
Thought 3: What appeared?
Would acting on it help or harm me or someone else?

Part B — Create distance

Choose one recurring thought and rewrite your relationship with it.

Original thought
Rewrite it using “I notice…”

Part C — Conscious ownership

Do I believe this because I examined it, or because I have repeated it many times?
If this thought was inherited, do I still consciously choose it today?
What would I choose to do if I did not automatically obey this signal?

Keep this worksheet. The purpose is not perfection. The purpose is awareness, examination and conscious choice.
