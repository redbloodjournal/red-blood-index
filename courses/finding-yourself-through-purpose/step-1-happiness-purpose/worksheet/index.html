Step 1 Reflection Worksheet

Is Happiness Really the Purpose of Life?

← Return to Step 1

This worksheet is for your own examination. There are no correct answers. Write the first honest answer that comes, then keep this page until Step 12 so you can compare what changed.

1. What do I currently believe the purpose of my life is?

2. Where did that answer come from?

3. Did I consciously choose it?

4. Would it still matter during a period when I was not happy?

5. What might purpose mean if it is not simply a feeling?

6. What is worth doing even when it does not make me happy?

Keep this worksheet. At the end of the 12-step course, return to these answers and compare what changed.
